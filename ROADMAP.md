# AstroManager roadmap

## v1.0.0 production release

AstroManager v1.0.0 covers the observing workflow from catalog search and
site selection through framing, planning, logging and backup. Release
validation covers Android, iOS, Web, Windows and macOS.

## After v1.0.0

- Prioritize release regressions in migrations, backup and restore, and
  platform-specific installation or navigation.
- Use reports from varied sites and devices to refine forecast-based
  recommendations, terrain coverage explanations and atlas resilience.
- Improve accessibility and keyboard workflows in chart, atlas and dashboard
  controls. Track performance on lower-powered and mobile devices.
- Review feedback on target discovery, planning and observation logs to
  prioritize usability improvements.

Follow [open issues](https://github.com/CodefrogCF/AstroManager/issues) and
[milestones](https://github.com/CodefrogCF/AstroManager/milestones) for
planned work and progress.
